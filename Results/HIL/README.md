# HIL Results

- `Bayesian/CompletedTopKCampaignV8/`: published Bayesian HIL data from the
  terminal portion of campaign V8.
- `Collaborative/CompletedTopKCampaignV8/`: published collaborative HIL data
  from the terminal portion of campaign V8.
- `CombinedTopKCampaignV8/`: analysis-ready combined condition, system-trial,
  robot-trial, failure, watchdog, and verification files for both missions.
- `AllocatorReplay/ActiveCampaigns/`: raw mixed campaign schedules, journals,
  reports, and logs.
- Other `AllocatorReplay/` folders contain preflight, regression, cohort,
  trace, device-backup, and legacy replay records.

The compact publications are tracked in Git. Raw call-attempt and call-phase
reports remain in the ignored campaign tree because the individual files exceed
GitHub's size limit.
